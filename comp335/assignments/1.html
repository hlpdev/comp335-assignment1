Projects:

Bloxopoly

A recreation of Monopoly built on Roblox using Luau inside of Roblox Studio. Releasing this Friday.

Features:

  • Matchmaking & Public / Private Lobbies
  • Full Character / Piece customization & monitization including:
    • Game Pieces
    • Face Textures
    • Hats
    • Music Kits
    • KO Effects
    • Titles
  • Customizable game settings & win conditions

Perch

A notch overlay for Windows. Music, calls, volume, and user widgets live in one pill at the top of the screen: present when something happens, gone when it's done. Written in C++20 on SDL3 and OpenGL, with the UI rendered by CEF (Chromium Embedded Framework) off-screen and composited over the desktop through a click-through layered window.

Zomboid Map

An interactive map for Project Zomboid B42, built to replace the unstable official map. A C++ ingest pipeline reverse engineers the game's binary map and texture formats and renders the entire Kentucky world into a isometric tile map.

NuAuth

A DRM and software licensing platform for solo developers and small teams.
Retired in 2025.

Build A Helicopter

An endless runner on Roblox. Fly a helicopter as far as possible before crashing or running out of fuel, then spend earnings across about 60 purchaseable parts covering blocks, propulsion, and aerodynamics.
Features procedural map generation with seeded random maps assmbled from section models, connected seamlessly at runtime, a full part shop, building, painting, a plot system, a tutorial, and cross-platform controls and UI.

COLD CALL

A Roblox adaption of COLDLINE by Adam Pype and Viktor Kraus. A Short horror puzzle where players navigate an automated phone system under a 10 minute timer to stop a nuclear strike.
Features a complex telephone interaction system and behavior tree, a live map system which shows missile trajectory, sound system, and multi-platform support.

Five Nights at Darius'

A multiplayer recreation of Five Nights at Freddy's 1 themed around Darius, an online personality. Players survive nights as security guards managing cameras and limited power against hostile NPCs.
Features a fully functional lobby / party system, full character customization, and an item shop.

Project Idea

One project idea I had was that we could create a campus marketplace website where students can buy, sell, or trade items like textbooks, dorm supplies, and electronics. Users can create accounts, post listings with photos, prices, and item condition, and browse or search listings by category. Buyers can message sellers directly through the site to arrange a meetup on campus.
